How do I get a key with model access?
  1. Ask your Laya administrator to open the Admin console.
  2. Under Users, the administrator creates or selects your user and clicks Issue key.
  3. They assign laya for text or laya-omni for text, images, audio, and video.
  4. Paste the one-time key below and click Connect. The model list will show only models allowed for that key.
If a model is missing, your administrator can open API keys and click Models beside your key to update access.
